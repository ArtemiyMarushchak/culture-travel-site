The Balkans: Sarajevo, Mostar, Kotor and Albania

A journey through three countries, read by its cities and roads: Ottoman Sarajevo, the Old Bridge of Mostar, the fjord of the Bay of Kotor, and Albania — from Rozafa Fortress to Skanderbeg Square in Tirana.

Route

  • Sarajevo
  • Blagaj
  • Mostar
  • Kravice
  • Kotor
  • Shkodër
  • Tirana

Duration

9 days / 8 nights

Countries

Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, Albania

The programme includes minivan transfers and tours with Russian-speaking guides, with entrance tickets to the places named below. Accommodation and budget are arranged separately, to your request.
Day 1

Sarajevo: Baščaršija and the Latin Bridge

Arrival in the capital of Bosnia and Herzegovina and a first walk through the old town.

Meet at Sarajevo airport and a private transfer into the city — about 30 minutes and 10 kilometres.

Sarajevo took shape where the Ottoman Empire, Austria-Hungary and Yugoslavia met. East and West stand on neighbouring streets, and you feel it at once.

The guided walk starts in Baščaršija, the old Ottoman bazaar of copper workshops and craft shops.

Along the way — Ottoman architecture and the Sebilj fountain, one of the symbols of the city.

Then the Latin Bridge, the place from which the twentieth century of European history began.

Night in Sarajevo.

Day 2

Trebević, the Yellow Fortress and the Tunnel of Hope

A day above the city and beneath it: the view from the mountain, and the passage by which Sarajevo lived through the siege.

After breakfast, a cable-car ride up Trebević. Sarajevo sits in the valley, mountains around it. This was an Olympic run; now it is a quiet viewpoint a few minutes from the centre.

The Yellow Fortress is one of the best lookouts: from here you can read how the city grew from the bazaar to the Austrian quarters.

In the afternoon, the Tunnel of Hope. During the siege this narrow passage kept Sarajevo connected to the world. The exhibition is short and very precise.

Return to the centre. Night in Sarajevo.

Day 3

Blagaj and the Old Bridge of Mostar

The road south: a dervish house at the source of the Buna, then the old town of Mostar.

After breakfast, departure towards Mostar. About 3 hours and 150 kilometres on the road.

A stop in Blagaj. The 16th-century tekke stands at the source of the Buna, directly under a limestone cliff. It is one of the strongest views on the route.

In Mostar, a walk through the old town, a UNESCO site: Ottoman houses, stone, and the Kujundžiluk bazaar with its copper shops.

The Old Bridge over the Neretva is the symbol of the city, rebuilt after the war. Night in Mostar.

Day 4

Kravice Falls and the Bay of Kotor

A morning at the cascades of southern Herzegovina, then a long road to the Adriatic.

After breakfast, a drive to Kravice Falls. Water drops in steps into green pools, with paths and viewpoints around them. There is time to walk and simply stand still.

Then Montenegro. About 5 hours and 240 kilometres, through the mountains to the Bay of Kotor: a fjord-like bay, steep slopes and old coastal towns.

Arrival at the bay. Night by the Bay of Kotor.

Day 5

The old town of Kotor

Venetian walls, stone lanes and a bay held for centuries as a sea gate of the Adriatic.

After breakfast, a transfer to Kotor and a tour with a local guide. The town is a UNESCO site: it sits deep in the bay, and for centuries that made it a port, not only a view.

Byzantium, Serbia and especially Venice show in the fabric. The walls are mostly from the 12th–14th centuries. Inside are churches, palaces and city gates.

If you wish, a boat trip on the Bay of Kotor.

Night in Kotor.

Day 6

A free day on the Bay of Kotor

A day without a fixed programme: the town, the water and the villages nearby.

After breakfast the day stays open. You can walk Kotor again at an easy pace, or go out to the coastal villages around the bay.

If you wish — a boat trip, a visit to an olive grove on the Luštica peninsula, or simply time by the water.

Night in Kotor.

Day 7

Shkodër and Rozafa Fortress

The drive into northern Albania, and a fortress above the place where the river meets the lake.

After breakfast, a private transfer to Shkodër. About 3 hours and 110 kilometres.

At the entrance to the city, Rozafa Fortress. Illyrians raised it; later walls saw the sieges of several empires. From the top, the valley and Lake Skadar open out.

Then a walk through Shkodër, the main city of northern Albania and a long cultural centre of the country.

If you wish — kayaks and birdwatching on Lake Skadar with a guide.

Night in Shkodër.

Day 8

Tirana

The capital of Albania: Skanderbeg Square, the clock tower, and a city that changed colour in the last decades.

After breakfast, a drive to Tirana, about 2 hours and 100 kilometres.

Tirana was grey and quiet for a long time. After the change of era it was repainted, built up and opened towards the mountains. Today an Ottoman mosque, Italian houses and new towers stand side by side.

A walking overview from the outside: Skanderbeg Square, the Et'hem Bey Mosque, the clock tower and the government buildings.

Night in Tirana.

Day 9

Departure from Tirana

A free morning and a transfer to the airport.

The morning in Tirana stays free, until the transfer to the airport — about 35 minutes and 18 kilometres.

The route ends where Albania opens towards the mountains: after Sarajevo, Mostar, the Bay of Kotor and Shkodër.
